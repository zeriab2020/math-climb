    Grown-up corner: progress report

    Questions follow the style of her school book's tests. Each topic has its own level from 1 to 10. Three first-try correct answers in a row move up one level. Two misses in a row move down one level. A wrong answer first shows a hint, then the worked solution. "Quiz" covers multiplying by a two-digit number, dividing a two-digit number by a one-digit number, and her book (Investigation 4 and Lessons 41–50). Every book quiz also mixes in 2 two-digit multiplication and 2 division problems. It never shows her lesson names. Each book unit has 100 problems from easy to harder, and each quiz gives her 20 of them with a timer. The app picks the unit itself: after 16+ right it moves to the next unit, otherwise it repeats with new problems. When she answers 3 in a row fast and right, harder challenge problems (5th–6th grade level) come in; two misses bring it back down. The "★ Challenge quiz" button gives 20 problems at that higher level, getting harder every 5 problems. A wrong answer shows an explanation of the book's method, never the answer, and she tries again; problems she cannot solve come back at the end. Every wrong answer, the correct answer, and her time are listed below. "Practice test" gives 20 mixed problems like a cumulative test and scores first-try answers. "Today's lesson" follows her school book's daily routine: Power Up (timed facts, count aloud, mental math a–h), New Concept, Lesson Practice, then 30 Written Practice problems that are checked at the end, followed by corrections.
