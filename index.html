    Grown-up corner: progress report

    Questions follow the style of her school book's tests. Each topic has its own level from 1 to 10. Three first-try correct answers in a row move up one level. Two misses in a row move down one level. A wrong answer first shows a hint, then the worked solution. "Practice test" gives 20 mixed problems like a cumulative test and scores first-try answers. "Today's lesson" follows her school book's daily routine: Power Up (timed facts, count aloud, mental math a–h), New Concept, Lesson Practice, then 30 Written Practice problems that are checked at the end, followed by corrections.
